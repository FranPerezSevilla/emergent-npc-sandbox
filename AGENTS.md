## Closure reason

M0–M3 demonstrated useful technical foundations: constrained NPC performance, separation of truth from belief, structured memory and relationships, provenance-preserving information propagation, and inspectable traces.

The first M4 human test exposed a substantially larger product problem: preserving semantic fidelity across unrestricted natural-language testimony, deception, implication and player-supplied premises would require either continuing phrase-specific fixtures and validators or moving to a more constrained dialogue-act/claim-realization architecture. The owner decided that this complexity no longer matched the desired project.
